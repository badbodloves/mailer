Create Lead Pool
A pool is a reusable lead list. Leads are marked as "used" after sending but never deleted. Import new leads anytime — duplicates are skipped and new ones are shuffled in between available leads.

How Pools Work

StateMeaning
PENDINGAvailable for the next campaign
USEDReserved/taken by a campaign, not yet sent
SENTSuccessfully sent
FAILEDSend attempt failed
When you select a pool in a campaign with e.g. 10,000 leads, the next 10k available (PENDING) leads are reserved. After the mailing, "Reset Used" makes them available again, or they stay marked so you know which leads have been contacted.
